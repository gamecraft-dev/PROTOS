How to play

Three rules

✥1 · Place glyphsDrag from your hand onto the loom, or tap a glyph then tap a cell. Everything you place stays for the whole run — the board is your machine.
➤2 · IgniteA beam leaves the source and travels in a straight line. Mirrors and vanes bend it. Each cell it enters costs 1 charge; when charge runs out, the weave ends.
✧3 · ChainEvery glyph the beam touches fires — up to three times each. Light × Weave is your score. Loop the beam back through your best glyphs to chain.

Miss the quota and the run ends. Hold all eight threads and the pattern is complete.
