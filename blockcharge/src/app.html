How to play

Three things

🧩Drag blocks onto the grid Three at a time. They don't rotate. When you've used all three, three more arrive.
✨Fill a full row or column It clears and you score. Clear more than one at once, or clear on back-to-back moves, and the points multiply.
⚡Every clear charges your meter Fill it and you pick a power — a bomb, a laser, or a fresh set of blocks. Powers wait in your slots until you need them.

It only ends when none of your three blocks fit anywhere — and even then, a power can dig you out.
